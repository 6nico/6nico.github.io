The problem

Reshop (items customers leave behind) piles up at Guest Services all day, and returning it usually means a dedicated walk across the store. That's paid time spent walking instead of helping guests.

What I built

  • A store model with zones, shelves and team-member trips
  • A route optimizer (Dijkstra + 2-opt) that finds the shortest path to put items back
  • Estimates of walking distance, time and labor hours saved
  • A side-by-side comparison of dedicated trips vs. grabbing reshop during trips people already make
  • 17 passing tests, and it runs in the browser with Pyodide

What I learned

The best labor saving isn't always the best plan: one approach saved the most time but left more items off the shelf at close, so the tool shows both numbers.
