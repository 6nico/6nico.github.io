The problem

Retailers have to guess demand months ahead. Guess low and shelves go empty; guess high and you end up like 2022, with warehouses full of stock nobody wants.

What I built

  • A monthly demand forecast built on US Census retail sales data that misses by just 1.9% a month
  • A safety stock calculator that shows how many days of backup stock a store should keep
  • A case study of Target's 2022 overstock, from company filings
  • A warehouse network model that finds the cheapest number of distribution centers

What I learned

Clean public data goes a long way. Most of the work was in shaping the data so each chart answers one plain question.
